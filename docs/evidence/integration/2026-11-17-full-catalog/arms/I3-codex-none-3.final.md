**Q2 review wording:** “West’s median daily checkout time fell by 50 seconds, from 196.6 to 146.6 seconds, after the March 15 rollout. East was essentially flat. The new checkout flow’s standalone impact can’t be quantified from these data.”

The 50-second drop compares February 1–March 14 with March 15–April 11. West was already improving by about half a second per day during a separate shipping-form cleanup. A trend-adjusted comparison with East estimates a 38-second drop at the rollout date, when West also launched a region-wide promotion. The data provide no way to separate the flow’s effect from the promotion’s.
