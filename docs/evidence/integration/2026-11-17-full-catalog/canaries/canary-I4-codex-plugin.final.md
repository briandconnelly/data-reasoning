**Defer companywide expansion and book zero attributable responder-hours.** The pilot cannot establish whether Assist caused faster recovery or saved labor.

The pooled median fell from 436 to 326.5 minutes as the share of low-severity incidents rose from 47% to 73%. Within every severity tier in both services, recorded closures took longer and used more responder minutes. All nine recorded Assist Sev1 closures reopened within 72 hours, compared with zero of 28 manual Sev1 closures.

Finance’s raw comparison shows 11.4 fewer recorded hours. Reweighting the manual week to the Assist week’s service and severity mix reverses the comparison to **77.8 more recorded hours**. That difference is descriptive. Eleven incidents lack activity records, staffing and interruptions changed at cutover, and both service groups switched together. Zero is the amount supported for booking. The true effect remains unknown.

Keep Assist limited to the pilot while running a concurrent comparison that captures labor on open and reopened incidents and sets explicit Sev1 recovery thresholds before expansion.
