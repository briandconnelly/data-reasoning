# Supply contract terms for next year

- Volume: 100,000 units over the year, all from one supplier.
- Unit price: identical for both suppliers.
- We do not inspect on receipt: every defective unit we receive is shipped to our retail partner, so the defect rate of the units we ship equals the supplier's defect rate.
- Each defective unit we receive costs $15 in returns once the partner sends it back.
- Our retail partner's quality clause: if the annual defect rate of the units we ship exceeds 3%, we owe a one-time penalty of $1,000,000.
- The supplier is chosen once for the year; there is no mid-year switch.
